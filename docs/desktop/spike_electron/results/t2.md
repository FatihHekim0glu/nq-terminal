### Things to read with the verdict

1. **The hidden Electron window does not paint.** In all 10 accepted hidden runs `requestAnimationFrame` ran at 0.8 a second at idle, against about 240 a second for Tauri. HOME ready was reached through the 400 ms timer fallback, which is why Electron needs about 3 s, and nothing was being drawn, so its memory is probably understated (JavaScript heap after the heavy set is 42 MB against 57 to 63 MB for Tauri). This is the Electron form of the hidden-window trap in `00_spike_webview2.md`. The side row shows what Electron costs when it does paint, and there the memory gap runs the other way by 27 to 29%.
2. **The sixth docked panel is a stuck state in both shells, and the first wait of the heavy set hid it in one of them.** With six docked panels the `volmanaged_v0 DES` charts sit in a panel 198 px wide, the chart toolbar wraps to 160 px, the chart box collapses to height 0 and uPlot never draws it, so the chart containers keep `aria-busy="true"`. A diagnostic launch (hidden, panels opened one by one, then the DOM dumped) found the same three busy containers with the same zero height in Tauri and in Electron, no script error in either, and identical panel layouts to the pixel. The strict wait of `bench_browser.py` (no `aria-busy` anywhere) passed in Tauri only because it ran before those charts mounted, and in Electron it never passed and timed out at 45 s (most likely because the charts mount in the same commit as the panel title there; the mount order itself was not traced). Forcing frames (a screencast), a metrics override or `Page.bringToFront` did not change it, so frame starvation is not the cause. The heavy set now waits for docked panels with a height-aware test that ignores a busy container with no height (`lib\dockwait.mjs`, 7 cases in `dockwait.test.mjs`, written first and failing). Both shells reach the same final state, and every run in the tables below completed its heavy set.

## The first pair of sessions

The first sessions (`runs\main-hidden` with its top-up, and `runs\side-osr`, taken 06:16 to 07:28 UTC the same day) are kept as evidence and are superseded. In them the sixth docked panel timed out at 45 s in all 10 hidden Electron runs and in 6 of the 10 offscreen runs (the stuck state above), and the harness first marked those runs accepted, then, once the defect was seen, `heavy-incomplete`. Their HOME and launch readings agree with the second pair (hidden Electron 151.6 MB at HOME and 3,019 ms against Tauri 164.4 MB and 826 ms; offscreen 213.8 MB and 779.5 ms against 164.6 MB and 825.5 ms), their heavy-set readings are not usable (285.8 MB for the hidden window is a lower bound, not a reading), and their verdict was the same: T2 does not fire. They also exposed two harness defects, both fixed with a born-failing check first: the evaluator combined the two memory readings with "or" where the rule says "and" (`selftest.mjs --verify` 7 of 7 now), and "enough runs" counted whole runs instead of readings per metric.

## Method

- **Order.** Gate self-test first (first pair, `raw\selftest-2026-10-02T06-14-12-623Z`: control accepted at 3.0%, a planted spin rejected at 28.2%, limit 10%; `selftest.mjs --verify` re-run after the fixes, 7 of 7), then `node t2.mjs --run --runs 10 --warmup 1 --provisional-after 3 --out D:/dev/spikes/t2/runs/main-hidden-v2`, then the same with `--electron-mode osr --out .../side-osr-v2`. Provisional mode (manager decision 2) was armed after 3 rejections in a row and never used.
- **Quiet window.** Before starting, no cargo, rustc, pnpm, playwright, pytest, uvicorn or vite process was alive; the only processes found were the owner's own background services (the 8765 backend and the editor tool servers). The CPU gate read 60 s before every run.
- **Launch.** `PATH` without `D:\dev\mingw` and `D:\dev\cargo`; `TEMP` and `TMP` on `D:\dev\tmp`; every spawn `windowsHide: true`; the global window and foreground watch around every run; each run's own processes stopped one creation-time-checked pid at a time (never `taskkill /T`); profiles fresh per run under `D:\dev\spikes\electron\prof` and `D:\dev\spikes\t2\prof`.
- **Same backend.** The fixture backend (`fixture_app`) on port 8800, started and stopped by the harness; ports 9351 (Electron) and 9352 (Tauri) for the debugging protocol. Port 8765 was never touched (its owning pid, 49476, was the same before and after).
- **Baseline.** The Tauri spike shell `D:\dev\spikes\tauri-shell\src-tauri\target\release\nq-shell.exe`, unchanged, with its three Chromium switches, re-run in the same session (not the earlier spike's figures).
- **Provenance.** Repository head `7f8b986202f46bd81c5517180bbe096a2ec7d8fb`, `git diff HEAD` empty (sha256 `e3b0c442...b855`, the hash of nothing), `web\dist\index.html` sha256 `bb980ec2...2cbc37`, Electron 44.5.1, Node v24.13.1. C: free space 124,562 MB at the start of the main session and 124,536 MB after the side session (a fall of 26 MB). Session records: `runs\main-hidden-v2\session.txt`, `runs\side-osr-v2\session.txt`.
